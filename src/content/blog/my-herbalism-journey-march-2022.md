We get Herbal Tea every morning!

**March 22, 2022:** Today, I start the Roots of Herbalism 8-month course at the California School of Herbal Studies. Jessie came up with me to help me get comfortable with maneuvering the Van for the next 8-months.

We are staying at Rainbow's End Farm this week. I heard that I need to meet Nan, who owns the land. It is beautiful and I love hearing the birds.

At school, it was mostly orientation and taking a long hike around the property. I can't believe that I get to learn this new craft at this school. Feeling mighty blessed right now!

**March 23, 2022:** I am noticing that I need a paper calendar. If you know me, you know that I like to weave technology into my workflow. So, not having access to my project management tool is very challenging for me.

Today, I picked up the required books for this course. We needed 5 books total. I have a feeling that I will be purchasing additional books for my Herbal library. There are several assignments we will be completing this semester. I have already reviewed the syllabus but need to figure out how I will keep my papers organized.

**March 24, 2022:** Last day of the first week complete! We learned about our first camping trip that is the 2nd week of April. I love to camp and now that we have this Van, I will be able to take this opportunity to use the Van for a whole week. There are some students who have never been camping before so they are in for a real treat.

We walked in the garden and met "Stinging Nettles", harvested them and enjoyed them in a Tea form. It was delicious, refreshing and grounding all at the same time. I definitely want to learn more about Nettles and how I can incorporate them into my life.
