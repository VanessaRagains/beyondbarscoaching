Life's journey often presents challenges that can be hard to understand. For many years, I grappled with my own hardships, particularly when I was separated from my family and friends as a child and placed in three different group homes over four years. Returning home at 17, I faced the daunting task of reintegrating into a world I barely recognized. I had graduated high school early and enrolled in community college, but the shame of being a "group home kid" made it difficult to connect with my peers. Soon, I found myself without a home again, feeling lost and unsupported.

During my time in group homes, I built a strong foundation, accountability, support, and tools that fostered my growth. My greatest challenge was losing that structure upon transitioning home.

Driven by my experiences, I launched Beyond B.A.R.S. Coaching, a program designed to help individuals transitioning from jail, prison, rehab, and group homes reclaim their lives and thrive. My research revealed a significant gap in resources for those re-entering society, leaving many at risk of recidivism — the cycle of returning to previous harmful behaviors. Without proper support, many individuals face an uphill battle after their release.

So, how does Beyond B.A.R.S. Coaching create a supportive environment that fosters lasting change?

**Mission:** Our mission is to empower individuals who have faced adversity — such as those transitioning from jail, prison, rehab, or group homes — to create and sustain meaningful behavior change. Through our **F.A.S.T. Method** (**F**oundation, **A**ccountability, **S**upport, **T**ools), we provide personalized roadmaps that guide clients toward discovering their career and educational goals. We are committed to fostering a sense of responsibility and well-being, emphasizing nourishment, self-care, and personal development to help our clients rebel against their adversities and build a life filled with direction and purpose.

**The F.A.S.T. Method**

1. **Foundation:** Identify your career and educational goals, focusing on nourishment, self-care, and personal development.

2. **Accountability:** Engage in monthly tasks tailored to your individual goals, complemented by one coaching call each month.

3. **Support:** Join weekly check-ins and access text-based coaching between our calls and events.

4. **Tools:** Benefit from a wealth of recorded videos, live-streamed events, and workshops designed to keep you engaged and motivated.

**What Will You Learn?**

1. **Discover Your Goals:** To enhance your education and career journey, receive a customized wellness plan, explore networking opportunities, and craft an impressive LinkedIn profile.

2. **Honor the 3 Pillars:** Tackle one primary concern while setting supportive goals in nourishment, self-care, and personal development.

3. **Develop a Healthy Mobility Practice:** Combine herbs and yoga to create a mobility practice that restores strength and flexibility.

**Program Bonuses**

1. Access to a library of 30-60 minute yoga videos across four styles.
2. Discounts on herbal remedies from my Apothecary.
3. Participation in a quarterly book club to support your sober journey.

By piecing together these elements, you can cultivate a life filled with purpose and direction.

**What Does B.A.R.S. Stand For?**

- **B**ehavior Change
- **A**mbition
- **R**esolve Recidivism
- **S**elf-Regulation

Since I was 19, I've sought holistic ways to heal from adversity. Beyond B.A.R.S. is the program I wished I had upon re-entering society in 2003. With the proper support, my transition could have been far less traumatic.

**Reaching Those Still Inside**

I'm also committed to supporting individuals currently in jail, prison, rehab, and group homes. The Beyond B.A.R.S. Workbook and Planner are designed to provide guidance and resources to those still inside. These workbooks serve as step-by-step guides, complete with addressed envelopes to encourage communication about progress or challenges. I'll offer snail mail coaching for anyone working through their workbook.

**Workbook Features**

*The Beyond B.A.R.S. workbook includes:*

- Sections for yearly, monthly, and weekly planning to help users track their appointments and build healthy habits.
- A step-by-step roadmap to empower individuals to rebel against adversity.
- Resources include yoga sequences, coaching exercises, and other supplemental materials.

After a year, participants will receive updated workbooks to maintain their momentum. If someone using the workbook is released, we'll connect via video call to ensure seamless support as they adjust to their new reality.

I am eager to visit jails, prisons, rehabs, and group homes to share how Beyond B.A.R.S. can empower individuals ready to foster healthy habits and prepare for life outside. Together, we can make a difference.
