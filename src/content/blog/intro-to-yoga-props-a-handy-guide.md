#### What You'll Learn:

Discover standard Yoga Props and alternatives to enhance your practice today!

#### **Yoga Block(s)**

**When To Use:** To support the space between your body and the floor, create joint comfort.

**Alternative Props:** Dictionary or atlas, folded blanket or towel.

**Asanas:** Placed between knees/hands (both if you have 2 blocks) in a **Chair**, Under your bent knee in 1/2 **Butterfly**, Under pelvis for **flexor stretch**, Between knees in **Bridge**.

#### **Yoga Strap**

**When to Use:** To hold you in a shape or pose, practice balance, and develop joint flexibility.

**Alternative Props:** Rolled-up towel, Neck Tie, Resistance Fitness Band (Be cautious of the snapback), belt.

**Asanas:** Explore **Reclined Leg Stretch** sequences, Seated **Forward fold**, and Standing **SLM**.

#### **Yoga Bolster**

**When to Use:** Under your knees, between your knees, under your back, or placed under your knees for padding.

**Alternative Props:** Pillows, couch cushions, rolled blankets, folded towels, meditation pillows.

**Asanas:** In **Savasana**, most **Yin poses**.

#### **Yoga Blanket**

**When To Use:** To offer warmth or comfort, to sit on, under your knees, under your back, under your head.

**Alternative Props:** Socks, if they don't hinder your practice, and extra layers like long sleeves and cozy sweatshirts (you can remove them).

**Asanas:** In **Savasana**, in place of blocks or bolsters.

#### **Yoga Towel**

**When To Use:** When your practice is sweaty to prevent slipping or falling and reduce injury, in place of blocks, bolsters, or straps.

**Alternative Props:** Neck Tie, Resistance Fitness Band (Be cautious of the snapback), belt.

**Asanas:** Explore **Reclined Leg Stretch** sequences, Seated **Forward fold**, and Standing **SLM**.
