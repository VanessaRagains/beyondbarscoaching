One of my favorite medicinal herbs — good ol' Dandelion. The Dandelion can grow anywhere showing its dedication to staying alive, dependability in their abundance (they are everywhere), and are disciplined plants that regrow stronger and stronger, despite being chopped down with a lawn mower multiple times!

Similar to the Dandelion, a Life Coach is someone you hire who is Dedicated, Dependable, and Disciplined enough to help you reach new healthy habits/goals and then maintain them. The value of hiring a Life Coach can be life-changing. I encourage you to keep these 3 D's in mind when you hire a Life Coach.

Before you continue reading, please understand that these are *my personal opinions and strategy that I use* when I am looking to hire a Life Coach. *Maybe* you will find them useful.

**DEDICATION:**

**Your Life Coach must be dedicated to your overall success.** You're a unique human and your program should reflect your unique journey including permission to adjust when needed.

**Your Life Coach must be dedicated to your progress.** This means taking initiative when they notice you need to pause and reflect on your situation before moving forward. Also, being an example of celebration and rest by taking time to celebrate your milestones and offering your body times to rest and recharge.

**DEPENDABILITY:**

**Your Life Coach must be dependable.** For me this means, I feel comfortable reaching out to them in between our calls. Before you hire a Life Coach, make sure you understand how they prefer you to communicate between your sessions. Being a dependable Life Coach also means responding to any client communications within a timely manner.

**Your Life Coach must have a dependable program that offers value** even *beyond* reaching your original healthy habits and goals. Feel free to ask your Life Coach how their program can support you after the duration is completed. Many Coaches offer a retainer program that can be used to simply maintain your momentum.

**DISCIPLINE:**

**Your Life Coach must display discipline.** This discipline is key in maintaining momentum without falling off track. How is their program structured for discipline? Your overall investment in the program shows a level of desire that leads to developing a trusting relationship with yourself practicing discipline.

**Your Life Coach must be able to adjust the program's level of discipline** from strict to lenient to find a unique balance while keeping you accountable to your new healthy habits and goals. Ask your Life Coach if they can add or remove certain features from your program.

**So, are you ready to hire a Life Coach?**

No matter what kind of Life Coach you are looking for, I believe these 3 D's can help you hire the right Coach for your unique journey.

**PS. Here are some powerful hiring questions you can ask that apply to the 3 D's I shared above: Dedicated, Dependability, and Discipline.**

1. How can you help me stay "*dedicated*" to my overall success in your program?

2. Describe how you practice "*dependability*" as a Life Coach in your program.

3. What does the level of "*discipline*" look like in your Life Coaching program?
