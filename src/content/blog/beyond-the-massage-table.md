Even before COVID, I had this desire to support clients beyond the massage table. I just couldn't quite figure out what that would be.

I love massage always have, always will, but I knew I had much more to offer!

**A little backstory…**

When I first moved up to the Bay Area, I did a ton of research on how to expand in my Massage career. I was overwhelmed by all of the many avenues for a dedicated and talented therapist, like myself.

In 2010, my work schedule gave me 2 weekdays off that I used for learning how to surf and brainstorming what I wanted to do in the next decade of my life. I borrowed my father's white board to map out my thoughts and ideas — I still have and use this white board today!

On it, I wrote "California School of Herbal Studies" along with some other courses that interested me nearby. This one was a full commitment of my time and energy. It required me to stay in the area during classes. After doing the math on what it would take with gas, hotel/hostel/renting a room, food and other expenses, I was not confident that I would be able to give this course the attention I knew it deserved.

**At this time in my life I was:**

- Still connected to an abusive relationship — why I moved to the Bay Area. Finally broke free in 2010.
- Smoking cigarettes — I quit in 2010.
- Drinking alcohol
- Poor eating habits — I ate out a lot!

I let a couple of those unhealthy choices go in 2010 (the abusive relationship and cigarettes), and I am so grateful I stuck with them!

**Reflecting On The Past Decade**

There were moments when I thought I'd never get the opportunity to attend this course until December 2020, literally a decade later, from writing that school on my whiteboard.

I experienced several shifts during this period in my life, like deciding to stop drinking alcohol on 12/9/2020 (a story for another time). That has been one of the toughest but powerful decisions of my life and has allowed me to be fully present to my emotions — no numbing. It was very clear how my mindset evolved from age 25–36 and how I needed to be in this space (alcohol-free) at this time in my life to have such a profound experience during this course.

I have found a new passion, a way that I can support clients beyond the massage table, in #Herbalism!

From here, I'd like to invite you to visit [beyondbarscoaching.org](https://beyondbarscoaching.org) and take a look around.

"I can officially call myself an #herbalist!" Being an herbalist is the perfect blend with #yoga and #lifecoaching. The knowledge I have is going to open many doors for me. Now it's my responsibility to continue to practice what I've learned so that I can perfect my new skills.

If you have any questions for me, please visit my [website](https://beyondbarscoaching.org) to send me a direct message!

I'm excited to hear from you!
