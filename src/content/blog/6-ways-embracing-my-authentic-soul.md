My "Word Hippo" app defines **embrace** as "the full acceptance of something". That is what I have gradually been doing this year. Getting to know myself, what I value, setting boundaries and standards of how I want to show up in this world — fully accepting who I am. Here is how I have embraced my authentic soul so far.

**6 Ways I'm Embracing My Authentic Soul:**

1. Studied #Herbalism for 8-months and lived part-time in our camper Van.
2. Finally started my Loc journey — more on this soon!
3. New Style of Dressing
4. New wooden Jewelry pieces — I never really found jewelry that I was drawn to until now.
5. Stayed Sober from alcohol since 12/9/2020.
6. Sticking to my boundaries which continues to strengthen the relationship that I have with myself.

**What I've Learned:**

- **Not everyone will agree with your choices and that is part of being an adult.** You get to make your own decisions. I started to ask myself if I will regret not experiencing "enter that something here". If the answer is yes, then I take steps in making it happen. I don't want to *regret* not doing something to make others comfortable anymore.

*I think I like this new me!*

- **The surge of happiness I feel showing up as my authentic soul is amazing.** Makes me wonder why I waited so long. My advice is rock that hairstyle you've always wanted! Take those Spanish lessons! Attend that festival! Whatever it is, when you are showing up as your authentic soul, it's always worth it.
- **New layers of your authentic soul will continue to unfold.** Meet them with curiosity and grace as they begin transform your life.
- **Like yourself first!** This one has been the most challenging for me. I have definitely fallen into the "will they still like me" mind trap before making a decision. I practice "Like Yourself First" to make sure that my decisions are based on what *my* mind and heart want. Not what others may or may not think of me.

There is an overwhelming amount of standards in our society. What to look like, smell like, dress like, how to wear your hair, what is sexy, how to *not* age, etc. it's exhausting! IMO, it's incredibly harmful to live by those standards.

I believe one of the most powerful decisions you can make against those standards is to embrace your authentic soul in *everything* that you do! It starts with a decision and then a commitment to yourself. Who knows, your decision may inspire someone to decide to embrace their authentic soul, too!

So pass it along!
