On December 9th, I celebrate six years sober. To mark it, Beyond BARS Coaching, the nonprofit I founded, is raising $6,000 on Giving Tuesday, December 1st. Here is why, where every dollar goes, and how you can be part of it.

## The moment it clicked

I left the group homes at seventeen. I went back to my family, and within a year or two, I was unhoused. I remember thinking: if I'd had a workbook like this, or a coach on my side to help me work through it all, it wouldn't have been so rough.

Years later, in my own recovery, I realized why I went through everything I went through. That realization became the workbook, *Your First 90 Days Beyond BARS*, built on the FAST Method: Foundation, Accountability, Support, and Tools.

I don't want anybody to navigate in the dark the way I did, trying to figure out what to do next after incarceration, after rehab, after the group homes.

## Why six years matters

Six years of showing up. Six years of choosing myself, one day at a time. The hard work of my recovery is paying off, and so is the volunteer work I do. I'm hearing back from the people I support, and I don't take that lightly.

When you push through adversity and keep going, even when people doubted you or you doubted yourself, you start to feel the growth. And you model it, so others can take a look at their own lives and change their relationship with alcohol without needing to numb in order to belong. I want everyone to have an easier path than I did.

## Where your gift goes

All $6,000 goes to workbooks for our cohorts. It is not split, and it is not a vague fund.

I facilitate every cohort online, for free, so there is no facilitation cost. Every dollar goes to the workbooks and to getting them into people's hands, through marketing materials, local events, and attending conferences.

A workbook is $22.99, so $6,000 is roughly 260 of them. Each is a twelve-week program with integration calls at weeks four, eight, and twelve, so nobody walks it alone. And we do buy one, gift one: when someone buys a workbook, they can give one to someone who needs it.

## Three ways to be part of it

1. **Give at [beyondbarscoaching.org](https://beyondbarscoaching.org/giving-tuesday),** any time up to and on Giving Tuesday, December 1st. Every gift counts at every size.

2. **Join me live on December 1st** at [beyondbarscoaching.org](https://beyondbarscoaching.org), in four short sessions (Pacific time): 8 am, noon, 4 pm, and 7 pm. Come for a few minutes. You don't have to give to be welcome.

3. **Share this post** with someone who believes in not only second chances, but fair chances.
