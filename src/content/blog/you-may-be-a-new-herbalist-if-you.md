This weekend, I told my partner, Jessie, I wanted to go to the Farmers Market nearby. We wanted to make a veggie stir fry and I wanted to make a broth. Our first stop was the empanada stand! We ordered our empanadas and then wandered through the aisles to see what was available.

Then something yellow caught my eye! Jessie and I walked to the stand at the very end of the aisle to find brown, reddish/pink, and yellow oyster mushrooms! I was delighted and other folks in the area must have felt my glee because they kept asking me what I was going to do with my yellow oyster mushrooms. Our plan was to add these to our veggie stir fry and then whatever we didn't use would go into the broth.

*[Image: Look at these gorgeous yellow mushrooms!]*

When we paid for the mushrooms, I asked the farmer if they had mushrooms every week. They said they would have them each week! Score!!

In this broth I had carrots, onion, lots of garlic, bell peppers, spinach, yellow oyster mushrooms, dandelion leaf (from our root harvest on the last week of school), and a green pepper (from the farmers market). I started the broth at 6:30 pm and didn't take it off the heat until 3:30 am (I set an alarm to make sure I turned the heat off).

*This is not necessary — I just wanted it to simmer longer than the time that I was awake.*

Once the broth cooled completely, I scooped out the veggies and placed them in a bowl. That went to the compost.

Next, Jessie helped me strain the broth so that there were no more bell pepper seeds. I have a beautiful pot my mother gifted me that I use to store the broth in. It stays in the fridge.

*[Image: Here is the color of the broth after it is strained.]*

From here, I only add salt & pepper to taste when I make each cup of broth. I heat the broth on the stove (I use a small saucer pot for this — it is the perfect size and amount for my mug). Do not use the microwave because it destroys all of the nutrients.

**BONUS:** If you live in or are visiting the Bay Area, there is a place called [**Trad Bone Broth**](https://www.tradbonebroth.com/) that you must try. I haven't been there *yet*, but am interested in their Bone broth lattes! I am pretty sure they use coffee in them!

I attempted to make one from my veggie broth using steamed oat milk without the coffee. I was very impressed! It was like a meal! I definitely feel more nourished when I am drinking broth on a regular basis. Plus, it is so easy to make!
