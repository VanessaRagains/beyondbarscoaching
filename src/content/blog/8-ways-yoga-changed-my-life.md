1) I was born with my feet internally rotated. Yoga keeps my tendons and ligaments aligned and healthy so there is no pain.

2) Yoga conditions my Diaphragm muscle for singing.

3) Prolonged my Massage career 17 years strong. I did not retire due to injury.

4) Keeps my lungs strong for teaching yoga, my HIIT training, SUP and surfing.

5) Because of my consistent Yoga practice, I didn't need ACL surgery after my snowboarding accident.

6) Helps me manage my Mental Health when I feel anxious or overwhelmed.

7) Had a successful recovery after my hysterectomy in 2019.

8) Yoga keeps all of our body systems in balance.
