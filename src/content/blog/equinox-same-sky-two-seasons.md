Tonight, September 22nd, is the equinox, one of two days a year when light and dark are almost perfectly equal.

Depending on where you're standing on this planet, that means two completely different things. For those of us in the Northern Hemisphere, autumn is beginning. For our friends in the Southern Hemisphere, spring is beginning.

Same sky. Same moment in time. Two different seasons.

That's one of the most honest pictures I've found for what it means to rebuild a life beyond bars.

## No Two Journeys Are the Same

No two people in this community are in the same place, and nobody is behind. You could be sitting in your harvest season, holding a job, a rebuilt relationship, a certificate that says you finished the program, while someone else is just planting their first seed. Both of you are exactly where you're supposed to be.

That's not a consolation prize. That's the truth of this work. Recovery, reentry, rebuilding — it was never a straight line, and it was never a race.

## The Harvest: What Autumn Can Mean on This Journey

For those of us in a harvest season right now, autumn asks us to gather what we planted.

Look at what your days of freedom have grown. A job. A reconnected relationship. Proof to yourself, on paper or in your own heart, that you can keep a promise to yourself.

Autumn also asks us to release. Trees let go of their leaves without shame, and so can we. What's a label the system gave you that you're ready to set down? What's an old story about who you "really are" that you're ready to stop carrying?

And autumn is preparation. The days are getting shorter, the holidays are coming, and for a lot of people rebuilding their lives, that stretch of the year is one of the hardest. This is the time to build your support system now, before you need it. Know who you're going to call.

## The Planting: What Spring Can Mean on This Journey

For our folks in the Southern Hemisphere, spring is just beginning, and that's its own kind of powerful.

Spring is about planting. What do you want to grow in this next chapter? A skill. A routine. A business. A promise to yourself. Seeds don't have to be big. They just have to go in the ground.

Spring is also emergence. Nothing blooms all at once. Some days it looks like nothing is happening. But notice the small signs: more confidence, a steadier routine, a laugh that catches you off guard because it's been a while.

## Balance

Wherever you are in the cycle, the equinox is asking all of us to check our own scales. Where are you giving too much? Where are you running on empty? Rebuilding a life takes real, sustained effort, and that effort has to leave room for rest, or it isn't sustainable.

## For Anyone Struggling This Season

Not everyone feels renewed this time of year. Reentry carries its own weight — old anniversaries, family tension, systems that still make simple things hard.

If that's where you are right now, you are not alone in it. Reach for your support system. Rest is part of the harvest too.

## A Few Questions to Sit With

- What have you harvested since you started this journey?
- What are you ready to release?
- What season does your journey feel like right now, and what does it need from you?

## Wherever You Are

Wherever you are on the wheel of the year, you belong here. The seasons keep turning, and so do we.

Stay rebellious, stay rooted.
