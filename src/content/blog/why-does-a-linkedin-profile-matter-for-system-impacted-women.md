### 1. It Rebuilds Professional Identity

When someone has been system-impacted, much of their identity has been defined *by what they've been through*.

**LinkedIn allows people to:**

- Reclaim their professional story
- Highlight skills, strengths, and growth
- Be seen for who they are becoming — not just their past.

This is identity repair, not just job searching.

#### 2. Many Jobs Are Never Posted Publicly

**A huge number of jobs are filled through:**

- Referrals
- Networking
- "Who you know" connections

**LinkedIn gives access to:**

- Hidden job opportunities
- Warm introductions
- Conversations that don't require explaining your entire past upfront

### 3. It Builds Confidence Through Visibility

**Creating a profile helps people:**

- Practice talking about themselves in a strengths-based way
- See their experiences as valuable, not shameful
- Build confidence before interviews even happen

Confidence grows *before* the job offer.

### 4. It Creates Distance From the Paper Resume

Resumes can feel rigid, limiting, and intimidating.

**LinkedIn allows:**

- More context
- Personal voice
- Ongoing updates as growth happens
- Skills and experience that don't fit traditional formats

It's a **living document**, not a static judgment.

### 5. It Supports Reentry With Less Pressure

**LinkedIn lets people:**

- Network before they're "ready."
- Observe workplace norms safely
- Learn professional language and expectations
- Engage at their own pace

This reduces overwhelm and nervous system overload.

### 6. It Shows Growth, Not Just Gaps

**System-impacted folks often worry about:**

- Employment gaps
- Education pauses
- Nontraditional paths

**LinkedIn reframes gaps as:**

- Skill-building periods
- Training, certifications, volunteering
- Personal development and recovery work

Growth matters more than perfection.

### 7. It Opens Doors to Second-Chance Employers

**Many employers on LinkedIn:**

- Actively support second-chance hiring
- Partner with reentry organizations
- Value lived experience and resilience

These employers are easier to find *on LinkedIn* than on traditional job boards.

### 8. It Helps Build Social Capital — Not Just a Job

**Social capital is access to:**

- Information
- Mentors
- Resources
- Opportunity

**LinkedIn helps people:**

- Build relationships over time
- Learn from others' paths
- Feel less alone in the process

This is especially powerful for people without family or professional networks.

#### 9. It Strengthens Accountability & Momentum

**Maintaining a profile encourages:**

- Consistent goal-setting
- Skill tracking
- Progress awareness
- Celebrating small wins

**It aligns beautifully with:**

- **F**oundation
- **A**ccountability
- **S**upport
- **T**ools

(FAST Method in action.)

#### 10. It Supports Long-Term Stability — Not Just the First Job

The first job after reentry is important —
but **career stability** is the goal.

LinkedIn helps people:

- Grow into better roles
- Change industries
- Advocate for themselves
- Avoid feeling "stuck" or disposable

This reduces burnout, relapse risk, and recidivism.
