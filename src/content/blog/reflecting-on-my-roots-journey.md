It's Monday, November 7th and I am taking today to reflect on the past 8-months during this incredible expansion of knowledge that I have gained during the Roots of Herbalism course! Yesterday, I completed all of my assignments and projects for this program. Each one necessary to help me learn this brand new area of knowledge. It was a goal of mine to complete all of these assignments — and I did!

Since March 22, I've been part-time living in a Camper Van making all of my meals using the cooking stove almost every day.

When I first started this program, I would leave on Monday night and drive home on Thursday. About a couple months in, I shifted to driving up early Tuesday morning around (6:30am) to arrive at Andy's before they open at 8am. I'd do my shopping for the week, use the restroom and grab a cappuccino before heading to class. School starts at 9:30 am sharp!

I stayed at Rainbows End Farm for the first couple of months. Unfortunately, the price each week was too much for me to maintain. Plus, it was a 20-min drive to and from school. I loved camping at site #6 and waking up to the roosters and the billy goats grazing on the nearby brush.

Then, I started staying at Mirabel Campsite near the Russian river for the remainder of the course. I didn't realize how this experience was going to impact my life.

I became very attached to campsite number 66. I slept under this incredibly unique redwood tree. It started as 1 large tree then decided to evolve into 2 trees. I believe this tree protected me every night I stayed there. During my stay, I developed a relationship with this tree.

*[Image: A picture of Vanessa having one more moment with the incredible redwood tree.]*

I learned in this program how to truly connect with the land, the trees, the plants, and in turn have created a deeper relationship with myself, my soul.

Building this relationship with this incredible tree, I decided to give an offering to show my gratitude. My offering was an herbal bundle with lemon balm, yarrow, peppermint and rosemary. Week after week, this offering seems to be becoming a part of the tree. Literally growing into the tree. This made me so happy to see. It's as if nature was accepting of this offering.

#### **So, how has my life evolved from my experience with Roots?**

**I have deepened my relationship with myself.** I trust myself to make healthy choices for my life. On December 9, 2020, I decided to quit drinking alcohol. This decision has been very challenging this year. Being present with my feelings, all of them, good and bad, has allowed me to process parts of myself that were silenced when I took a drink. I made a promise to myself that I will no longer be silent and be a voice for myself and others who may not have found the courage to do so for themselves, yet.

**Developing an understanding of our connection to mother earth.** I have always been aware of the detrimental destruction that has become of our planet. This course has opened my eyes up wider to the very real reality of how important it is for me as a new herbalist to share this knowledge to my family, friends, and community. I feel a deep responsibility to support my clients to truly heal in a holistic manner.

**New friends!** Making friends has been an area that I struggle with. Finding deep meaningful relationships is important to me so I continue to seek out those types of connections. Within my group of intelligent classmates, I really developed lasting relationships with 3 unique souls. Each of them have supported me throughout this program when I felt like I wanted to quit. Never made me feel "dumb" for asking questions I didn't know the answer to. Each of them offering their friendship in return. I will cherish these connections forever!

This Thursday, I will receive my certification of completion for the 8-month intensive Roots of Herbalism. I've got a lot of studying to do so that I can be the best herbalist I can be. This knowledge and experience is a gift and I promise to never forget it!
