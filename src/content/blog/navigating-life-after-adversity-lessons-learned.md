When I came home from my last group home, I thought the transition back to my family would be easy. But life had other plans. Not long after returning home, I was kicked out of the house. While it was painful at the time, I now recognize that this was a pivotal lesson in my life. There were many challenges and setbacks, but those experiences ultimately led to profound personal growth.

During this tumultuous period, I was fortunate to have a wise and compassionate junior high school counselor who took me in for nine months. She became a mentor and a safe space, someone I could confide in. When she eventually retired and moved back to Iowa, I found myself struggling to navigate life without that support. At 19, I bounced between unstable housing situations, from couch surfing to sleeping in my car. Eventually, I found a job that allowed me to rent my own apartment for $750 a month. From there, I packed up my life and moved to the Bay Area, seeking new opportunities and stability.

Through all these experiences, I learned valuable lessons about creating structure and stability in my life. Now, as part of my Beyond BARS Coaching program, I want to share the strategies that helped me build resilience and maintain a sense of direction during uncertain times. These are tips I've personally used, and they've helped me stay grounded through even the toughest transitions.

### 1. **Establish a Routine**

Creating and sticking to a routine was one of the hardest things I had to learn on my own. At first, I struggled to stay organized and on top of my commitments. Eventually, I turned to paper planners to track my appointments, events, and to-do lists. As technology advanced, I transitioned to using my phone's calendar — it's an invaluable tool to maintain consistency. The key is to establish a rhythm in your day, no matter how small the steps may seem. Structure breeds stability, and that consistency will help you move forward with confidence.

### 2. **Take Steps Toward Your Career or Educational Goals**

One of the most powerful questions you can ask yourself is: *What do I want to do with my life?* Whether you're aiming to further your education, develop new skills, or dive into the workforce, it's important to start building your roadmap now. Together, we can create a clear path forward and establish actionable steps toward your career or academic goals. As part of the Beyond BARS Coaching program, I help clients build professional profiles on LinkedIn — an essential tool for networking and career development.

This is not just about finding a job, but about developing a lifelong network and relationships that support your personal and professional growth.

### 3. **Build a Support Network**

The people you surround yourself with matter. Early in my journey, I realized that I had to be intentional about the people I hung around. It's easy to get caught up in negative influences, especially when your judgment is clouded by difficult circumstances. But over time, I learned to set boundaries and prioritize my well-being. You must become your own biggest advocate.

Building a solid support network is critical to navigating life's challenges. This network could include friends, family, mentors, or online communities. As part of my coaching program, I offer access to an online support group where you can connect with others on a similar journey. Numerous educational institutions, mentors, and organizations can also provide valuable resources. We can help you tap into these networks to ensure you have the support you need.

### 4. **Practice Yoga: The 8 Limbs of Yoga**

Yoga played an instrumental role in my own transformation. It wasn't just about physical exercise — it was an internal dialogue with myself. Yoga taught me to trust my decisions, find strength, and develop the discipline to follow through on my goals. The 8 Limbs of Yoga offer a holistic framework that can guide you toward greater peace, balance, and clarity.

In my program, I incorporate the 8 Limbs of Yoga into our daily practice. These guidelines help cultivate self-awareness and mindfulness, which are key components in any personal transformation.

### 5. **Journal, Draw, or Write Poetry**

Expressing yourself creatively — whether through journaling, drawing, or poetry — can be incredibly therapeutic. When I came home, I found that writing became my release. Music was my constant companion, and I wrote over 30 poems reflecting my emotional journey. I poured out my pain, my anger, my sense of abandonment, and also my hope. Writing helped me process complex emotions and make sense of my experiences.

Journaling or drawing has profound effects on the brain and nervous system. It's a powerful way to express emotions, explore thoughts, and clarify goals. The Beyond BARS Coaching program offers weekly opportunities for creative self-expression, encouraging you to explore different outlets to express your truth.

### 6. **Use Music as a Tool for Healing**

Music has always been a source of healing for me. In the group homes, access to music was a *privilege that had to be earned*, but once I had it, I used it as an escape, a way to process my emotions, and a tool to ground myself. Even now, I have curated playlists that help me navigate challenging times.

I encourage you to create uplifting playlists or explore genres like classical music, which can promote relaxation and focus. Music has the power to shift your mood and energy, so use it to support your emotional well-being and keep you centered.

### 7. **Research and Utilize Available Resources**

One of the most important lessons I learned was the need to rely on myself, even when I had support. While outside resources are valuable, ultimately, you are the one who must take action to achieve your goals. Research is key — whether it's about learning a new skill, exploring career options, or finding support organizations.

The Beyond BARS Coaching program is designed to guide you through this process, helping you build a foundation of accountability, support, and the right tools to succeed. Together, we'll create a personalized roadmap using my F.A.S.T. Method — Foundation, Accountability, Support, and Tools — to ensure you have what you need to move forward.

### 8. **Read Personal Development Books**

Reading has always been a source of growth and inspiration for me. Whether you listen to audiobooks or read traditional print, personal development books are a great way to gain new perspectives and learn from others' experiences. My bookshelf is filled with books on topics ranging from herbalism to activism to yoga philosophy, and I continually seek out resources that expand my knowledge and understanding.
