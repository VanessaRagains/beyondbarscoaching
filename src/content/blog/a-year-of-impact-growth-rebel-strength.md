Hi Rebels —

As 2025 comes to a close, I've been reflecting on what an incredibly powerful year this has been for **Beyond BARS Coaching**, for the sober community, and for the movement we're building together. This year stretched me, grew our programs, expanded our reach, and opened doors I never imagined possible when this mission first began.

Here's a look at what we've been up to — and where we're headed next.

#### Hope House: Two Full Cycles of Transformation

In 2025, I had the honor of leading the **12-Week First 90 Days Beyond BARS curriculum** *twice* with the women at Hope House. Together, we worked through the FAST Method — Foundation, Accountability, Support, Tools — and applied the 3 Pillars of Nourishment, Self-Care, and Personal Development to rebuild structure and stability after recovery.

This fall, we stepped into a brand-new curriculum:

### Chaos to Calm: Regulating Your Nervous System Without Alcohol

This 12-week program blends science, neuroplasticity, and trauma-informed coaching to help participants understand exactly *why* they react the way they do — and how to create emotional regulation without turning to alcohol.

The conversations, breakthroughs, journaling, and vulnerability in this group have been incredible.

#### Connecting With the Sober Community Offline

I attended a sober picnic this year and it opened a beautiful door into a community of people choosing sobriety with strength, joy, and connection. From there, conversations led me to the **Reentry Fair & Expo**, where I gave a 10-minute presentation and handed out all 25 Beyond BARS Coaching flyers — face-to-face, heart-to-heart.

This event allowed me to meet leaders in reentry, rehabilitation, and support services — including Voices of Recovery.

#### New Partnership: Voices of Recovery

Because of the connections made at the Reentry Fair, I reached out to **Voices of Recovery** and am thrilled to begin offering **The First 90 Days Beyond BARS** to their community starting in January.

This is a huge milestone — expanding our work beyond a single program and into additional partner organizations.

#### Workbook v2: Almost Complete!

Version 2 of the Beyond BARS Coaching Workbook is in the final stretch. This updated edition includes:

- Enhanced exercises
- Nervous system education
- More reflection prompts
- A new design & layout
- Better alignment with both the First 90 Days and Chaos to Calm series

This will be a cornerstone of our 2026 expansion.

#### Sober Rebel Podcast: Born in 2025

This year, I launched the **Sober Rebel Podcast**, blending science, sobriety, nervous system education, and empowerment for Rebels on their healing path.

Episodes reflect the heart of what we teach inside the programs — compassionate self-leadership, body-based healing, and learning to live without alcohol.

#### The Sober Rebel Club for Women on Meetup

In 2025, I also launched an entirely new community space for sober women:

**The Sober Rebel Club for Women**

Built on connection, belonging, and movement, this Meetup offers:

- HMB Walks + Hot Chocolate
- SUP (Stand-Up Paddleboarding) Days
- FREE Botanical Garden Outings
- Trailhead Hikes + Stretch Sessions
- BYOT Nights (Build Your Own Tacos)
- Movie Nights
- Weekly Virtual Coaching, LinkedIn/Resume Labs, and Sunday Reflections

And with new members joining already, it's clear this group will become one of the most meaningful offerings inside Beyond BARS Coaching.

#### Program Metrics: A New Collaboration for 2026

This year, I connected with a **Forensic Psychologist** who offered to help me gather **formal metrics** for the Beyond BARS Coaching programs. These metrics will help track each participant's growth, including:

- Completion of weekly coaching exercises
- Emotional regulation improvement
- Boundary development
- Relapse risk indicators
- Skill-building progress
- Readiness for reentry or employment

These data points will be essential for future grants, donor trust, and expansion into facilities.

#### Goals for 2026: The Big Vision

Looking ahead, here's what's next:

**1. Get all Beyond BARS content on EDOVO tablets, including:**

- Workbook curriculum
- Podcast episodes
- Yoga videos
- Breathwork practices
- Reflection questions
- Sober Rebel resources

This will give justice-involved individuals direct access to tools they can use right inside their facility.

**2. Grow the Sober Rebel Club into a Bay Area movement**

In-person events, consistent meetups, connection, and sober community — every week.

**3. Host quarterly fundraisers**

To support sustainable growth and allow more participants to join at no cost.

**4. Expand trauma-informed training + partnerships**

To more reentry programs, recovery centers, and transitional living environments.

We're just getting started.
Here's to a powerful, calm, rebel-strong 2026.
