*This entry discusses some experiences with alcohol that may be triggering to some readers.*

**Today, I am celebrating 2-years sober from alcohol.**

While I am celebrating this milestone, you must know that this has been one of the most challenging decisions I've made for me. I'd like to share how I set myself up for success.

I'll start with my decision to stop drinking alcohol. Honestly, I've played with this idea to quit off and on. Especially when I've woken up hungover. Seriously contemplating when I was studying to become a Yoga Instructor back in 2012. Drinking felt hypocritical being part of the wellness field. But I continued to drink.

Fast forward to 2020. I was drinking heavily that year. I had to make some hard decisions with my business. I found myself with too much time on my hands. I filled it by completing my Yin Yoga, Yoga-Shred and Social Media courses. Once those courses were completed, I still had all of this free time. Time for some reflecting and tough questions.

As I shared earlier, I've played with the idea of what life would look like if I gave up alcohol. I had plenty of concerns mostly about what might shift for me and my partner, how social events would be, and how I would feel living a sober lifestyle. Sometimes being the only one who was sober in the room.

**I had to be honest with myself and answer the following questions.**

- Why am I considering getting sober from alcohol?
- Why is living this lifestyle important to me?
- What are Pros to this choice?
- What are Cons to this choice?

THEN, I explored these questions.

- What would change in my current relationship?
- How would I feel at social events?

But 1 question seemed to keep holding me back from moving forward. *Do I trust myself to commit to this change?* It is literally all around me. It's in our home, at every grocery store we shop at, on every other TV commercial (especially during the holidays), and every single event. Everywhere.

**Let me share what has helped me along the way.**

I committed to living alcohol free on December 9, 2020. Two years later, I am so grateful to arrive at this part of my journey. I have learned so much. Creating a game plan and being *honest* with myself meant creating boundaries that would set me up for success.

**Defining Success with Boundaries**

- Communicating what I needed super-duper clearly to my partner, family, and friends.
- Creating a support system of people who *fully* support this change, not just tolerate it. There *is* a difference. Also, including being mindful of who I follow on social media channels.
- Being able to *change* your mind. (ie: at first you want to go to an event and then you decide that you don't, later.)

**Building A Trusting Relationship With Myself — basically getting to know myself again.**

- Expressing my emotions out loud and paying attention to how my body reacts to certain experiences and/or people.
- Celebrating Milestones and taking them seriously! Yes, they are a big deal!
- Showing up in this world as my authentic soul.
- Lots and lots of self-care and love!

**What I've learned about myself so far after 730 days sober:**

- My emotions are *very* intense! There has been a lot of tears the last 730 days, but those tears have been numbed for such a long time. I let my body move through the emotions as it needs to. This includes yoga, crying, journaling, stating how I feel to someone out loud, or just being present in the emotions that come up, etc.
- I have some strong beliefs when it comes to human rights, our environment, and supporting our indigenous communities that I want to take an active role in!
- I do not believe in "gender roles". Period.
- I have to be in constant communication with myself, being present with how I am feeling at all times. Lots of self-care and love.
- I am building a trusting relationship with myself — very empowering!

So, here's to 730 days sober from alcohol and many more milestones to celebrate in the future!
