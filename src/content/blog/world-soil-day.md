**Why Give Credit To Our Soil?**

- 95% of our food comes from soils.
- 18 naturally occurring chemical elements are essential to plants. Soils supply 15.
- Agricultural production will have to increase by 60% to meet the global food demand in 2050.
- 33% of soils are degraded.
- Up to 58% more food could be produced through sustainable soil management.

*Source: [Trello FAO Board](https://trello.com/b/PZAW2Vb9/fao-soils-content)*

Another class, we hand mixed our own seed balls.

**Seed Ball Benefits:**

- Disperse seeds into areas for regeneration and repopulation of plants.
- Many herbs work to increase habitat
- Are beneficial by attracting pollinators.
- Can build soil.
- Useful in sensitive landscapes, shallow soils, forested areas

*Source: Bryan Bowen, CSHS 2022*

Here is the recipe that Bryan gave us to make our own seed balls each year to restore and nourish the soil for the following year.

**Recipe**

*If using NATIVE SOILS you will want to sift in advance*

**Mix**

- 5 parts clay
- 1-3 part compost or potting soil
- 1 part seed
- Slowly add water to form thick dough like consistency

*These can be formed into any shape — triangles, balls, pyramids*

*Source: Bryan Bowen, CSHS 2022*

**Seeds To Add To Seed Balls:**

Yarrow, Chamomile, Fennel, Dandelion, Plantain, Comfrey, to name a few.

*Source: Bryan Bowen, CSHS 2022*
